Executive Summary

This infographic presents a comparative empirical evaluation of three prominent open-source large language models—Llama 3, Mistral, and Gemma 2—tasked with extracting relational knowledge graphs (semantic triples) from highly specialized Legal and Medical corpora. We analyze the critical trade-offs between information yield, computational latency, and cross-domain adaptability based on a mathematically verified corpus size of N=10 documents per domain.

There is a stark divergence in processing footprints between domains. The Legal corpus exhibits significantly higher structural syntax complexity, demanding double the processing latency of the Medical corpus, yet yielding a substantially higher density of explicit semantic associations.
