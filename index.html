Conclusion & Recommendations

  • →
    Real-time Operations: Utilize Llama 3 for workflows where latency is a strict hard constraint, accepting lower yields in complex technical domains.
  • →
    Offline Deep Mining: Deploy Gemma 2 for batch-processing legal or structural text where maximum extraction recall overrides computing time concerns.
  • →
    General Purpose & Versatility: Adopt Mistral as the Pareto-optimal standard. It balances speed and yield effectively and possesses the highest zero-shot adaptability across divergent domain vocabularies.
